Historical q8 longitudinal audit · frozen-model audit

The historical longitudinal model retains 1024 feature coordinates but restricts patch-to-patch variation to only 8 smooth spatial components. Those components capture a median 16.7% of within-slide UNI variation (range 9.3%–24.5%); fitted reconstruction R² is 0.167. Median coefficient retention after posterior shrinkage is 99.0%. Most loss therefore occurs in the spatial projection, before the additional shrinkage.

With the same frozen longitudinal scorer, replacing latent inputs with raw UNI changes patch ranks: median correlation 0.475, with 0/5 shared top patches. This is an input-sensitivity check, not a refitted prediction model.

Incoming-column attention agrees with an explicit dense calculation; patient 250’s 64-draw export reproduces exactly. Posterior-mean attention versus the displayed 64-draw average has median rank correlation 0.999996 and 5/5 shared top patches. These checks point to strong latent compression as a leading explanation, rather than an aggregation-direction or export error.

Weight contrast also differs: median relative spread (SD / mean weight) is 0.511 for longitudinal, 1.518 for TANGLE and 0.044 for direct self-attention. The direct weights are close to uniform even when their top-ranked patches look better. Heatmap colors show ranks, not the size of weight differences. The historical methods also differ in scorer width, loss, epoch and review sessions; this comparison does not isolate a single cause.
